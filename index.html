Proactive Robot Behavior

Research project · Cornell University

“Can a robot pick up on what people mean, not just what they say, and help before it's asked?”

Most of my projects go through the same three steps.

Spark

I notice something that bugs me. Why does Alexa forget what I told it yesterday? Why do I never look at my trip photos again? I write it down.

Ship

iOS · Jun 2026 → now Private beta · 6 users

Pixory

An iOS app that turns a trip's worth of photos (around 300) into a captioned highlight reel told in story order. It groups photos by time and place, scores each one on 9 visual dimensions, and sends the top 30 to GPT-4o to pick, order, and caption. When you swap photos, it adjusts those 9 weights, so after about 5 uses it starts choosing the way you would.

Alexa+ agent · Apr–May 2026 ★ Best Design · 70+ teams

ContextIQ

A memory layer for Alexa+. It pulls in conversations from a Bee Pioneer wearable and brings up relevant history when you ask for something vague, saving up to 5 follow-up questions. Retrieval is a Graph RAG pipeline on Bedrock and Neptune that combines semantic search with graph expansion and reranks with Cohere. A Nova Sonic voice agent uses that context across 8 tools.

Codex skill · 2026 Open source · MIT

Slide to Lesson

A Codex skill that turns PDF lecture slides into an interactive lecture video, with narration, animations where a concept needs one, and quizzes between sections. You write narration per slide in JSON. It generates the speech, trims animations to fit, and builds a chaptered MP4 and an HTML player that pauses for the quizzes. Everything runs locally unless you choose an external voice.
